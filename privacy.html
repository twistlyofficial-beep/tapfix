Privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026.

What we collect

  • Request form: the name, business, website address, email or phone and message you type. We use it only to run your tap test and reply to you.
  • Site visits: Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies and without identifying you.

Where it's kept

Form submissions are stored with Cloudflare, our hosting provider. We don't sell or share your information, and we don't add you to a mailing list.

Deleting your information

Ask through the request form on our homepage and we'll delete what we hold about you.

Our video uploader

We run a small private tool, "Rich or Broke Uploader", that uploads videos to our own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is used only by us, only for our channel, and stores its access token as an encrypted secret. It does not access anyone else's YouTube account or data. Its use of Google data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page.
