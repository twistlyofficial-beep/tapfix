Terms of service

Last updated November 18, 2026. Plain-language terms for TapFix website repair work.

The free tap test

We load your website the way a phone does and check every call button, contact link and form we can reach. The test only looks. It changes nothing on your site and costs nothing.

Quotes

If we find a problem, we send a written quote with a flat price before any work starts. Current prices are on our homepage. Nothing is billed unless you approve the quote in writing (a reply email is enough).

Payment

  • After you approve a quote we send an invoice through a card payment provider. We never ask for card numbers by email or phone.
  • Single fixes are billed 50% up front and 50% when you confirm the fix works. Larger projects follow the schedule in their quote.
  • If we can't fix the problem we quoted, you are not charged for that problem and any deposit for it is returned.

Access to your site

We need editor access to your website builder (for example WordPress, Wix, Squarespace or GoDaddy) or an introduction to whoever manages your site. We never need your bank, email or domain-registrar passwords. Tell us when the work is done and we'll ask you to remove our access.

Delivery

Most single fixes are done within 48 hours of getting access. Before we mark a job finished we test the fix from a phone and send you what we checked.

What we don't promise

We fix specific, tested problems. We don't promise more calls, sales, search rankings or any other business result.

Care plans

Optional monthly plans ($49 or $99) re-run the tap test each month and fix small contact problems we find. Cancel any time before the next billing date.

Questions

Use the request form on our homepage and we'll reply.
